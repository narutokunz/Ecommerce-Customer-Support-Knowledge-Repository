
# Refund Policy

## Purpose

This document stores information related to refunds in the e-commerce business system.

## Refund Processing

- Refunds are generally processed within 5-7 business days after approval.
- The actual time may vary depending on the payment method and financial institution.
- Customers can contact support if the refund is not received within the applicable period.

## Payment Issue

If the payment is deducted but the order is not confirmed, the customer should check the order status and contact customer support if necessary.

## Knowledge Rules

IF a return is approved,

THEN initiate the refund process according to the applicable policy.

IF payment is deducted but the order is not confirmed,

THEN guide the customer to check payment and order status.
